Ryzen 5 9600 fits the B850 board’s AM5 socket.

Before ordering the starter PC

Three checks every new builder should make.

These quick checks reduce the chance of choosing the wrong variation on a retailer page.

Product variation

Match the exact model and option

Amazon listings may combine multiple capacities, colors, heatsink versions, or hardware revisions. Confirm the selected option matches the product described on this page.

Memory setup

Install the RAM in the recommended two slots

Use the motherboard manual’s two-stick configuration, normally A2 and B2, then enable the correct EXPO profile only after confirming the PC boots reliably.

First boot

Connect the monitor to the graphics card

After assembly, connect DisplayPort or HDMI to the RTX 5070 rather than the motherboard video outputs, then install current chipset and graphics drivers.
