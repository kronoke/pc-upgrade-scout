About the project

Clearer PC buying decisions.

PC Upgrade Scout is a starter affiliate website focused on helping gamers choose upgrades based on compatibility, use case, and value—not just a list of product links.

Editorial approach

Every published recommendation should answer four questions: Who is this product for? What systems will it work with? What are the tradeoffs? Is there a better choice at another budget?

What comes next

Replace the placeholder product tiers with researched products, add original photos or diagrams where useful, publish detailed guides, and connect the retailer links to approved affiliate accounts.
